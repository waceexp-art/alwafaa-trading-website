USA & LIBYA MARKET REQUIREMENTS

ASTM, EN & Applicable Cement Standards

Al-Wafaa Trading targets the United States and Libya for Egyptian cement and clinker export. The required standard depends on the product, buyer, project and destination-market requirements. We coordinate with the relevant Egyptian manufacturer to provide the applicable technical specification and available certificates.

United States — ASTM Focus

  • ASTM C150/C150M — Portland Cement
  • ASTM C595/C595M — Blended Hydraulic Cements
  • ASTM C1157/C1157M — Performance Specification for Hydraulic Cement
  • ASTM C109/C109M — Compressive Strength of Hydraulic Cement Mortars
  • Other ASTM test methods and requirements as specified by the U.S. buyer or project

ASTM International lists C150, C595 and C1157 among the principal hydraulic-cement standards. The exact current edition and applicable requirements should be confirmed for each U.S. transaction.

Libya — Market & Standard Focus

  • Libyan requirements applicable to the imported cement product
  • Applicable Libyan National Center for Standardization and Metrology requirements
  • EN / European specifications where required by the buyer or project
  • ASTM specifications where expressly required by the buyer or project
  • Manufacturer's technical data, test reports and certificates of conformity as applicable

For Libya, the exact applicable national specification should be confirmed with the Libyan importer and the competent authority before quotation or shipment.

Information to Include in an Inquiry

  • Target market: USA or Libya
  • Cement product or clinker
  • Required quantity in MT
  • Packaging: bulk or bags
  • Destination country and port
  • FOB / CFR / CIF requirement
  • Required delivery date
  • Required standard: ASTM, EN, Libyan standard or other specification
  • Technical specification and certificate requirements
