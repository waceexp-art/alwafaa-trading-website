OVERVIEW

Established in 2020, Al-Wafaa For Trading is an Egyptian international trading company specializing in the export of high-quality cement and clinker to global markets.

We connect international buyers with established Egyptian cement manufacturers and support the export process from sourcing and competitive pricing to documentation, logistics coordination, and delivery.

Our focus is to build long-term business relationships through dependable supply, clear communication, and efficient export solutions from Egypt to international markets.

Al-Wafaa For Trading works to provide international buyers with a professional and straightforward connection to Egyptian cement supply.
