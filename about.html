OVERVIEW

Established in 2020, Al-Wafaa For Trading is an Egyptian international trading company specializing in the export of high-quality cement and clinker to global markets.

We connect international buyers with leading Egyptian cement manufacturers and support the export process from sourcing and competitive pricing to documentation, logistics coordination, and delivery.

With experience in international cement trade and more than 1 million MT of material handled during our activities, we provide reliable sourcing and professional support to buyers worldwide.

Our focus is to build long-term business relationships through dependable supply, clear communication, and efficient export solutions from Egypt to international markets.
