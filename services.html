OUR SERVICES

Export Support
from Source to Delivery

Al-Wafaa For Trading provides professional export support for international buyers sourcing Egyptian cement and clinker. We connect buyers with established Egyptian manufacturers and help identify suitable products according to specifications, quantity, packaging, and destination.

We coordinate the key steps between buyers, manufacturers, and shipping partners, including product sourcing, commercial offers, packaging, export documentation, and shipping arrangements. Our objective is to make the export process clear, efficient, and well organized from the initial inquiry and quotation through shipment coordination and final delivery.

WHAT WE DO

Product Sourcing

We connect buyers with established Egyptian cement manufacturers and help identify suitable cement or clinker products according to specifications, quantity, packaging, and destination.

Competitive Export Solutions

We coordinate with manufacturing partners to develop competitive offers based on product requirements, quantities, shipment terms, and destination markets.

Packaging & Loading

We support suitable packaging and loading arrangements, including bagged and bulk cement, according to product requirements and shipment conditions.

Export Documentation

We coordinate required commercial and export documentation, including invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, quality documentation, and other documents required for international shipments.

Shipping & Logistics

We coordinate shipment planning with relevant logistics and maritime partners, supporting port arrangements, vessel or container planning, and delivery coordination.

Customer Support

We maintain communication throughout the transaction, from initial inquiry and quotation through documentation, shipment coordination, and final delivery.
